Before you go

Can I transfer my tickets?

Yes. Tickets live in “Your tickets” and can be sent to a friend up to 24 hours before doors open.

What if the concert is rescheduled?

Your tickets stay valid for the new date. If you can’t make it, you can ask for a full refund within 14 days of the announcement.

How do I book accessible seats?

Pick any zone marked accessible on the seat map, or contact our support team and we’ll find the best spot for you and a companion.
